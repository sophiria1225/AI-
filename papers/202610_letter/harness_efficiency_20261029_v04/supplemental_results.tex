\noindent
This supplement accompanies the v04 letter draft. It reports a closed
development comparison on four known tasks (model seed 257, world seed 42).
It is separate from the positive P123 and P64/P66 studies in the letter.
The original two executions (session S0) and ten continuation executions
(session S1) are linked without hiding the user-requested shutdown.
The second S0 execution was evaluated once from its saved state after resumption;
neither S0 execution was regenerated.

\noindent
Task slots 01--04 denote photo operations, settlement, refund, and CSV processing.
RECURSIVE (TR), DELTA\_OBJECT (APP), and DELTA\_UPDATE (UPD) denote ordinary
recursive summarization, append-style state, and explicit state-update requests.
All three modes succeeded on 3/4 tasks; all failed on CSV. The TR and UPD CSV
runs reached the API cap. APP CSV stopped during a model call at the wall-time
limit and retains one unknown-usage call.

\noindent
The separately defined TR--UPD comparison has known usage in all eight cells.
UPD uses 389,395 more tokens (+74.54\%) over all four tasks and 79,762 more over
the three common-success tasks. These exact pairwise differences do not fill
the null three-mode totals or the missing APP comparison differences.
Three known-usage focus calls requested explicit updates, but zero updates
were installed and zero changed old-state items reached subsequent known-usage
actor calls. No efficacy or novelty claim follows. The split server sessions
also prevent interpreting this as an isolated causal effect of the mechanism.
