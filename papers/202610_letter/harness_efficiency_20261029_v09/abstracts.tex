\begin{abstract}
LLMエージェントの履歴要約と終了判断を独立に評価した．既知課題の成功を保つ総トークン数の削減を観測した一方，同時要約は別呼出し反復より高費用だった．専用要約を含む全モデル呼出しの計測により，方式間の費用差を示した．
\end{abstract}

\begin{eabstract}
We independently evaluated history summarization and termination control for LLM agents. Total tokens decreased while success was retained on known tasks, whereas simultaneous summarization cost more than separate repeated summarization. Measuring all model calls, including dedicated summarization, revealed cost differences between methods.
\end{eabstract}
